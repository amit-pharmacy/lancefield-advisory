About Lancefield Advisory

Lancefield Advisory is an insolvency and restructuring advisory firm based in Glasgow. We advise company directors, sole traders, partners and individuals who are under financial pressure and want to understand where they actually stand before they commit to anything.

Book a free, confidential call

Why the firm exists

The insolvency and debt advice market has a trust problem, and it is not a secret inside the profession. Much of the advice given here comes from people paid out of the procedure they recommend, either from the appointment itself or from a product carrying a commission. The question of whether you need that procedure is being answered by someone with a stake in the answer.

The pattern is familiar. Liquidation proposed where a turnaround was still possible, a formal arrangement sold to someone who needed a conversation with their bank, a director pushed towards a default answer before anyone has looked at what they signed personally. The harm is rarely a shortage of options. It is the absence of an unbiased read on them while there is still time to use them.

Lancefield was set up as the counterweight. We look at the whole position, business and personal together, and say what we think even when it earns us nothing.

The experience behind the advice

The work here is led by someone with over 20 years in restructuring, gained across three of the Big Four accounting firms. That is a background in the difficult end of this work: lenders who have run out of patience, HMRC arrears that have been rolling for a year, directors who signed a personal guarantee and have not slept properly since.

We advise across every sector, though three come through the door more than the rest: hospitality and leisure, construction, and the supply chain behind both. That concentration works in your favour, because we can usually tell you in a first conversation whether what you are seeing is ordinary for your sector or a sign of something structural.

Why we take no appointments

Certain roles in a formal insolvency can only be held by a licensed insolvency practitioner. Liquidator, administrator, nominee and supervisor of a company voluntary arrangement, and in Scotland trustee in a sequestration or under a protected trust deed. Lancefield holds none of them and does not seek them.

Where a formal appointment genuinely is needed, we bring in a licensed practitioner we know and trust, and stay alongside you through it rather than handing you over at the door. Because nobody here takes the appointment, we earn nothing from one happening, which leaves us free to say when a formal procedure is the wrong answer. Most firms in this market are not in a position to write that sentence.

What we believe

Six principles run through the work. They were written down before this website was, and they decide the awkward cases.

Integrity first.
The advice has to be right for the client, including when it is uncomfortable to give or less profitable to act on
Clarity over complexity.
Confusion does real damage here, and plain explanation is most of what gives someone back a sense of control
People before profit.
Vulnerable situations call for restraint, not a sales process
Results through realism.
Honest outcomes rather than false hope, and no promises about what a procedure will achieve
Follow-through.
Responsibility does not end when the advice is given. It runs through to resolution
Specialism over breadth.
We do not try to do everything. Where a specialist firm will serve you better, we refer, and we stand behind who we refer to

That last one is why our finance and funding and accountancy pages introduce a trusted specialist firm rather than pretending the work happens in house. Not a panel. Not a broker pool.

The Clarity Path

People hesitate because they think asking a question starts something they cannot stop. It does not. You keep control of the pace, and nothing formal happens without your say so.

Confidential consultation.
A first conversation to understand the situation and any deadlines already running against you. Free, and it commits you to nothing
Diagnostic review.
The full picture, business and personal at once, so nothing important surfaces late. Where a lender or a board needs it in writing, that becomes an independent business review
Clear, unbiased options.
Every realistic route in plain English with its consequences attached, from turnaround and debt restructuring through to liquidation where that is honestly where things stand
Supported implementation.
If action is needed we handle the mechanics, deal with creditors and HMRC, and bring in a licensed practitioner where the law requires one
Ongoing support.
We stay involved after the decision, through recovery and onto steadier ground

Acting before you are forced to is what most changes the options left to you, which is what contingency planning is for.

Where we work, and what we cover

We are based in Glasgow, and our team works across Scotland, England and Ireland, so where the business sits is rarely the obstacle people expect. The differences between those jurisdictions do matter, and we are careful with them. Personal insolvency in Scotland runs on its own statute with its own regulator, and a firm working from English precedent will get parts of it wrong. Our Debt Help Scotland section covers the Scottish personal routes properly.

The work divides into four areas. Restructuring is what a business does while it still has choices. The insolvency side covers the formal procedures, including administration where a viable company needs protection while a rescue is arranged. Debt Help Scotland handles the personal side, which for our clients is frequently the same problem wearing different clothes. And financial advisory covers valuation, forensic and transaction work.

Some clients prefer to sit across a table, others would rather use the phone. Both are fine, and you can contact us whichever way suits you.
