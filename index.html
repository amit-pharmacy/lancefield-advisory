About Lancefield Advisory

Putting People Before Process Since Day One

The insolvency industry has a problem. Too many firms earn their fees from specific outcomes — pushing IVAs because they're profitable, recommending liquidation when restructuring is possible, or overlooking personal exposure because it's not their department.

We saw too many business owners receiving conflicting advice from multiple advisors, none of whom had the full picture. Lancefield Advisory was founded to be different: we are the safe, ethical first point of clarity for people under financial pressure.
