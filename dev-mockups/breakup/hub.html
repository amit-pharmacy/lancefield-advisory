Debt help in Scotland

Scotland has its own debt solutions, its own legislation and its own government agency running them. If you have been reading about IVAs, bankruptcy petitions or Debt Relief Orders, that is English law and most of it does not apply to you.
Book a free, confidential call

Start here: free advice exists, and it is good

Citizens Advice Scotland, StepChange and National Debtline are debt charities offering free, impartial money advice across Scotland. A money adviser at any of them can issue the certificate you need to apply for your own sequestration, and an approved money adviser is a statutory requirement for that application in any case. If your situation is straightforward, free money advice may be all you need, and we will say so rather than take a fee for repeating it.
These charities are not a lesser option. Their money advisers deal with more debt cases in a month than most commercial firms see in a year, and the support they offer costs you nothing. Use them.
Where a paid adviser earns their place is in the more tangled cases: a director whose company debt and personal debt are entangled, someone with a property and equity to protect, a person with assets or income that make the standard route the wrong one, or anyone who has been given advice that does not sound right and wants a second view.

The four routes, and what each one does to you

Every option below is a formal Scottish process. Each has real consequences, and the differences between them matter enormously depending on whether you own a home and what your income looks like.

Debt Arrangement Scheme

The most common route for an insolvent company. The directors start it voluntarily, shareholders pass a resolution, and a licensed insolvency practitioner is appointed as liquidator to wind the company up in an orderly way. Choosing a CVL rather than waiting to be forced into liquidation generally reflects better on the directors' conduct.

Read more about CVL

Protected trust deed

You pay a monthly contribution, assessed against what you can actually afford, normally for 48 months, and the remaining unsecured debt is written off at the end if you keep to the terms. A protected trust deed treats your home differently from the way sequestration does, which is why it is often the more protective route for a homeowner. "Protected" means enough creditors accepted it that the rest are bound too, and until a trust deed becomes protected it does not stop them acting.

Sequestration

The Scottish term for bankruptcy. Your assets pass to a trustee who realises what can be realised, and the debts included are written off. There are two routes in, and the simpler Minimal Asset Process is built for people on a low income with no property. It is the most serious option and not the first one we reach for, but for some people it is genuinely the right one.

An informal arrangement

Not everything needs a statutory solution. Sometimes the answer is a repayment plan, some breathing space, and a conversation with creditors who would rather be paid slowly than not at all. This is where free advice services do excellent work.

The Accountant in Bankruptcy is the Scottish Government agency that administers sequestration and the Debt Arrangement Scheme and maintains the public Register of Insolvencies. Its website sets out the current thresholds, which do change.
One term to be careful with: a debt management plan is not a Scottish statutory solution. It is an informal arrangement, usually run by a commercial debt management firm regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, and it does not freeze interest or stop creditors the way a debt payment programme does. Some are perfectly reasonable. They are simply not the same thing, and the names are close enough to confuse.

Which debts these cover

Most of what people owe can go into a Scottish debt solution: credit cards, overdrafts, personal loans, catalogue and store accounts, payday lending, council tax arrears, and money owed to HMRC.
Council tax arrears deserve a particular mention, because in Scotland councils can enforce them quickly and aggressively, and they are a very common reason people reach this page. They are included in these processes like any other unsecured debt, which is often a relief to hear.
What does not go in are secured debts, so mortgage arrears sit outside all of this and have to be dealt with directly with the lender. Court fines and, generally, student loans are also excluded. If you are receiving Universal Credit or another income-related benefit, that affects which route you qualify for rather than whether you qualify at all, and under the Minimal Asset Process it can be part of what gets you in.

Solvent

Assets exceed liabilities, and the directors can declare the debts will be paid in full with interest within a stated period of up to twelve months. Route: MVL. Outcome: funds returned to shareholders, often with a favourable tax treatment.

Insolvent

Cannot pay debts as they fall due, or liabilities exceed assets. Route: CVL if you act, compulsory liquidation if a creditor acts first. Outcome: assets realised, proceeds distributed to creditors by statutory priority.

If you are genuinely unsure which applies, that is exactly the question a short diagnostic conversation settles.

How to tell which one fits

The honest short answer is that it depends on three things: whether you own property, whether you have surplus income after essential expenditure, and how much you owe relative to both.

You own a home with equity, and you have some surplus income.

A protected trust deed is usually the first thing to look at, because your home is handled differently than it would be in sequestration

You can clear the debt in full given more time, and enforcement is the pressure.

The Debt Arrangement Scheme freezes interest and stops creditors acting, without writing anything off or risking assets

You have little income, few assets and no property.

The Minimal Asset Process route into sequestration exists precisely for this, and it is quicker and cheaper than the alternatives<

Your debts are mostly the company's, and you signed personal guarantees.

Deal with the company first, or at least at the same time, because a guarantee will keep generating personal liability until the company position is resolved. Start with liquidation or, if a creditor has already gone to court, with winding up petition
Anyone who tells you the answer before asking about your home, your income and your guarantees is selling rather than advising.
A money adviser will work through this with you properly, using a standard affordability assessment that every Scottish debt option is built around. That assessment is what determines whether a debt payment programme is realistic, what a protected trust deed contribution would be, and whether you fall inside the Minimal Asset Process. It is the same arithmetic whoever does it, which is another reason free debt advice is worth taking before you pay anyone.

If you are a company director

This is the situation we see most, and the one that free debt advice services are least equipped for. The company is struggling, you have signed personal guarantees on the bank facility or the lease, and the two problems have become one problem.
Solving them separately rarely works. Put the company through a liquidation without addressing the guarantees and the lender simply turns to you. Sort out your personal debts while the company keeps trading at a loss and the exposure grows again behind you.
What is needed is a view of both at once: what the company procedure does to the guarantee, what the guarantee then does to you, and which order to take them in. That is the crossover our insolvency practitioner section deals with on the business side, and this section deals with on the personal side.
One point worth stating: you cannot act as a company director while sequestrated without the court’s permission. If continuing to run a business matters to you, that alone may decide which route is right.

What debt help does not do

Three honest limits, because the promises made in this market are sometimes extraordinary.

It does not remove secured debt.

A mortgage or any other secured lending survives every one of these processes. The security holds

It does not stop a creditor with a personal guarantee.

The guarantee is a separate contract and it is enforced separately

It does not repair your credit file quickly.

A sequestration stays on file for at least six years from the date it is awarded. For most people at this point the damage has already been done by missed payments long before

What it does do is stop the position getting worse, put a defined end date on it, and hand the creditor pressure to someone whose job it is to absorb it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Scottish debt help really different from England?
Substantially. Sequestration is not the same as English bankruptcy, a protected trust deed is the Scottish counterpart to an IVA, and the Debt Arrangement Scheme has no English equivalent whatsoever. The legislation is different, the administering body is the Accountant in Bankruptcy rather than the Insolvency Service, and the timescales differ. English guidance found online will mislead you on the detail.
Will I lose my home?
Not automatically, and the answer depends heavily on which route you take, how much equity there is, who else owns the property and whether the mortgage is being paid. A homeowner should take advice specifically on this before applying for anything, because the routes treat property very differently.
Does it cost anything to talk to you?
No. The first conversation is free, confidential and commits you to nothing. If free advice elsewhere is the right answer for you, we will tell you that in it.
Will my employer find out?

Most people keep their jobs and most employers never know. Some professions and roles have restrictions, so check your contract and any professional body rules. Sequestration is recorded on the public Register of Insolvencies, though few employers go looking.

How long does it take to be free of the debt?
It depends on the route. The Minimal Asset Process discharges automatically after six months. A full sequestration can discharge from twelve months. A trust deed normally runs 48 months. The Debt Arrangement Scheme runs until the debt is repaid in full, which can be considerably longer, but nothing is written off and nothing is at risk.
Can I choose which debts to include?
Largely no. These are collective processes, and creditors are treated according to statutory rules rather than your preferences. Paying one creditor ahead of others before a formal process starts can itself cause difficulties later.
